YOUR FIRST RUN

Three habits before the first jump.

These are general player recommendations, not claims about hidden game systems.

01

Read the room

Pause before a crowded jump. Another player can turn an easy platform into the risky part.

02

Take clean angles

Center your camera, line up the landing, then commit. Rushed corrections cause more falls than slow setup.

03

Expect the hug

Treat nearby players as moving hazards without assuming they are hostile. Keep space when the ledge is narrow.
