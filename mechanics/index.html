Know what is confirmed—and what is not.

Live games evolve. We label official claims, editorial guidance, and unknown details so you can use this wiki without confusing a tip for a game rule.

CONFIRMED BY LISTING

Hug, throw, and troll

The official Roblox description explicitly presents these interactions as part of the game and frames the top floor as the destination.

PLAYER ADVICE

Space is a resource

Leaving room around other players is our recommendation for reducing unpredictable contact near narrow platforms.

NOT VERIFIED

Exact technical rules

We do not state hug range, cooldowns, throw strength, checkpoint locations, or reward conditions without a current reliable source.

The core loop

Based on the official description, the broad goal is simple: climb the tower, deal with chaotic social interactions, and reach the top. The listing also refers to a “FREE Admin CARPET,” but wording on a game page can change. Check the official experience for the current reward presentation and requirements.

Hugging and throwing

The names of these interactions are confirmed; their exact implementation is not documented in the sources we reviewed. Treat other avatars as dynamic obstacles, keep a safe landing buffer, and verify controls in the live game rather than relying on an old video.

Updates and level changes

The official description says updates can add levels, tools, and new throw moments. That is why this guide avoids pretending the tower has a permanent floor count or a fixed obstacle order.

Account safety

No legitimate guide needs your Roblox password, browser cookies, or a downloaded “code generator.” Use only Roblox’s official site or app to launch the game.
