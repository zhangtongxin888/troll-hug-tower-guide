FAQ

Is this the official wiki?

No. This is an independent fan guide and is not affiliated with Roblox or Rush Entertainment!.

What is the goal?

The public description asks players to climb to the top while hugging, throwing, trolling, and competing with friends.

Does the game have codes?

We have not verified a current official code system. See our codes page.

Why are there no live player stats here?

They change constantly. We prefer stable facts that can be audited.
